Sail along with the first great European voyages. The map starts with the Portuguese capture of Ceuta in 1415 and follows the ships down the African coast, around the Cape of Good Hope, to India, across the Atlantic with Columbus, and finally around the world with Magellan and Elcano, who return in 1522. Press play and watch the routes open up one landing at a time.

Colors show who sent the voyage. Green is Portugal, red is Spain and blue is England. Use the layers to compare how the three powers expanded and see how different their routes were.

Click any point to see what happened there, such as a first landing, a fort founded or a captain killed, and the date. Points sit on the place named, such as a port, cape or island.

Some locations are debated or only approximate. For example, historians still argue over which Bahamian island Columbus reached first, and the sites of La Navidad and Balboa's view of the Pacific are marked as approximate. Notes say how precise each date is.
