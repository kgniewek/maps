This map follows the Napoleonic Wars across Europe, from the cannonade at Valmy in September 1792 to the final exile on Saint Helena in October 1815. When you press play, the timeline moves forward through the years and battles, sieges, treaties and key political moments light up where they happened. Long events such as sieges and multi-day battles stay visible for as long as they lasted.

Each color marks one phase of the conflict. The first four layers follow the Coalition wars in order, from the First Coalition in blue through the Third and Fourth Coalitions in teal and green. The Peninsular War in Spain, Portugal and southern France has its own layer, as do the Fifth Coalition of 1809, the Russian Campaign of 1812, the Sixth Coalition of 1813 and 1814, and the Hundred Days of 1815. Layers overlap in time, so you can filter them to follow one theatre of war at a time.

Click any marker to see the name of the event, the modern country it lies in, a short line of context such as who won, and how precisely the date is known. Battles with a start and end date, such as Leipzig or Eylau, show the full span.

Please treat the data as a careful overview, not a complete record. It covers the best-known battles, sieges, treaties and turning points, and many smaller actions are left out. Every date here is known to the day, but coordinates mark the nearest town or the middle of the battlefield and can be off by a few kilometres. Sea battles are only approximate. Several events were fought over wide areas, so one point is a simplification.
