Watch the deadliest pandemic in European history advance city by city. It starts at the Crimean port of Caffa in 1346, reaches Sicily in October 1347 on Genoese ships, and by 1353 has gone all the way to Moscow. Press play and the pins appear in the order the plague arrived, with ports like Messina, Marseille, Venice and Bristol lighting up first and inland cities following.

Colors show the wave. Dark red is the first Mediterranean ports, then red for 1348, orange for 1349, yellow for 1350 and blue for the last Russian and Baltic cities. You can use the layers to follow a single year and see how fast the disease moved.

Click any city to see what is known about its outbreak, such as how many died or which ship brought it. Each point sits on the city center.

Medieval chronicles rarely give exact days, so each date shows how precise it is (month, season or year). Dates for smaller towns are estimated from the regional wave.
