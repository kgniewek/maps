Follow almost two centuries of crusading across Europe, the Mediterranean and the Middle East. The map starts in Clermont in 1095, where Pope Urban II called the First Crusade, and ends at the fortress of Atlit in 1291, the last crusader stronghold to fall. Press play and the key sieges, battles and treaties appear in order, from Nicaea and Antioch to Jerusalem, Hattin and Acre.

Colors show the stage of the story. Dark red is the First Crusade, orange the early crusader states, yellow the Second Crusade, green the Third, blue the Fourth, purple the Fifth and Sixth, pink the Seventh and Eighth, and gray the fall of the crusader states. Use the layers to follow one crusade at a time.

Click any point to see what happened there, such as a siege, a defeat or a treaty, and the exact date. Points sit on the city or battlefield.

The map shows selected events, not every battle. Some places appear more than once because they changed hands or hosted several key moments, such as Acre and Constantinople.
