This map follows 153 of the best-known volcanic eruptions from ancient times to 2022. Press play and the eruptions appear one by one in the order they began, so you can watch the focus of volcanic disasters move from Italy and Iceland to Indonesia, the Americas and the Pacific.

Each eruption is colored by the period it began in. Pale yellow marks everything before 1600, yellow 1600-1799, orange 1800-1899, red 1900-1949, purple 1950-1999 and deep violet 2000-2022. You can filter by these layers to look at a single era.

Click a marker to see the volcano's name, the country, a short description of what happened, and how precisely the date is known: day, month or year.

Please treat the data with care. Each point marks the volcano's summit area, not the exact vent, and coordinates are approximate. Many eruptions lasted days, months or years, so the date shows when the famous phase started, not when it ended. Where only a month or year is known, the date is set to the first of that month or year. The list is a selection of notable eruptions, not every eruption in history.
