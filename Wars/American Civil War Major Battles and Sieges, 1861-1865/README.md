This map follows the American Civil War from the shelling of Fort Sumter in April 1861 to the last fighting in May 1865. When you press play, the battles and sieges appear in date order, so you can watch the war spread from the Virginia front and the Mississippi Valley to the Atlantic and Gulf coasts, Georgia, the Carolinas and Texas. Long engagements such as sieges stay active for as long as they lasted.

Colors show the four main areas of fighting. Blue is the Eastern Theater (Virginia, Maryland and Pennsylvania), red is the Western Theater (from Kentucky and Tennessee down the Mississippi and through Georgia and the Carolinas with Sherman), green is the Trans-Mississippi (Missouri, Arkansas, the Southwest and Texas), and purple is the Coastal and Naval war. Use the layer filter to show only one of them.

Click a marker to see the country, who won, and how exact the date is. Outcomes follow the commonly used assessments of each battle, and some, such as Antietam or the Wilderness, are listed as inconclusive because historians do not agree on a clear winner.

This is a selection of the major battles and sieges, not every engagement of the war, which had thousands of actions. Dates are exact to the day, with multi-day battles showing their first and last day. Coordinates mark the battlefield or the nearest town or landmark, so a marker shows the area of the fighting rather than the exact positions of the lines.
