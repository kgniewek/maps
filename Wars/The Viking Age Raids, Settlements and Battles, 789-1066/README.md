This map follows the Viking Age from the first recorded landing in 789 to the battles of 1066. When you press play, places and events appear one after another in the order they happened, from monastery raids in Britain and Ireland, to winter camps of the Great Army, to settlements in Iceland and Greenland, to Rus campaigns toward Constantinople and the Caspian.

The colors show five periods, moving from light yellow for the earliest raids to dark red for the final decades. The layers match them: first raids (789-850), the Great Army (851-900), settlement (901-950), Atlantic and Rus expansion (951-1000), and kings and the end of the age (1001-1066). You can filter by layer to watch one period at a time.

Click any marker to see its name, the modern country, a short note on what happened, and how precisely the date is known. A precision of day means the date is firmly recorded, month or season means only that part is reliable, and year means the event is placed on 1 January of that year for the animation.

This is a selection, not a full list of every raid or battle. Early sources are sparse and often disagree, so many dates are only known to the year, and some locations are traditional or approximate, such as the sites of Edington, Assandun and Brunanburh. Dates for the early Rus come from later chronicles and should be treated as traditional.
